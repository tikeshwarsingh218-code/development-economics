### Psychology and economics

Psychology and economics studies how human behaviour differs from the assumptions of standard economic models. Attention, beliefs, preferences, limited information, and behavioural biases can influence economic decisions.

The field can help explain choices involving saving, education, health, labour supply, and public policy. Behavioural insights can also inform the design of interventions intended to improve individual or social outcomes.
