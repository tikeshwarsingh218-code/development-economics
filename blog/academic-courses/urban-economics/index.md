### Urban economics

Urban economics studies how people and firms locate across cities and how economic activity is organised spatially. Housing, transportation, land markets, agglomeration, and local public services are central topics.

Cities can generate productivity benefits because firms and workers interact more frequently in dense locations. At the same time, congestion, housing costs, pollution, and unequal access to services can create important urban challenges.
