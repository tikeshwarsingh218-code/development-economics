### Structural transformation and economic growth

Structural transformation concerns changes in the sectoral composition of an economy as productivity and incomes rise. Workers and resources may move from agriculture into manufacturing and services, although the pattern differs across countries.

The process is closely connected to productivity growth, urbanisation, migration, and changes in labour demand. Understanding why transformation occurs at different speeds is an important development economics question.
