### Can Better Information Change How Citizens Engage with Government?

Giving people information is often assumed to improve their ability to participate in public life. But information has to be understandable, credible, and relevant before it can affect behaviour. A citizen may receive a detailed explanation of a government programme but still be unable to use it if the information is difficult to interpret or does not answer the questions that matter to them.

Generative AI makes this question particularly interesting. AI systems can potentially turn complicated administrative information into simpler explanations, but they also create concerns about accuracy and interpretation. I am interested in whether the format in which government information is presented can affect trust, understanding, and civic engagement.
