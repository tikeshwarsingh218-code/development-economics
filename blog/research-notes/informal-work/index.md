### Why Is Informal Work So Persistent?

Informal work is not simply a temporary stage on the way to a formal job. For many workers, informal employment is a long-term way of earning a living. Workers may value the flexibility of informal work, while employers may value the lower fixed costs and flexibility that informal arrangements provide. At the same time, workers can face limited social protection, uncertain incomes, and weak bargaining power.

This makes informality an interesting development question. I am interested in understanding when informal employment reflects a worker's choice, when it reflects a lack of alternatives, and how firms and institutions shape these decisions. Looking at informality through both the worker and employer side seems particularly useful for understanding India's labour market.
