### What Makes a Job a Good Job?

A job is more than the wage attached to it. Workers may care about whether they are paid on time, whether the employer treats them respectfully, how predictable the work is, and whether they can rely on the employer to honour promises. These features can be particularly important in informal labour markets where formal contracts provide limited protection.

Thinking about job quality from the worker's perspective also changes how we think about labour-market matching. Two jobs with similar wages may not be equally attractive if their working conditions are very different. I find this useful for thinking about both worker choices and the incentives firms face when trying to attract and retain workers.
