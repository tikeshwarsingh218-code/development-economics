### Development Questions in the Himalayan Region

Mountain regions face a particular combination of economic opportunities and geographic constraints. Distance from major markets, difficult terrain, limited connectivity, and exposure to environmental risks can affect access to jobs, education, healthcare, and markets. At the same time, tourism, agriculture, natural resources, and migration create important sources of income.

I am interested in the Himalayan region partly because development cannot be understood separately from geography. Migration can connect mountain households to opportunities elsewhere, while tourism and local services can create employment closer to home. These interactions raise broader questions about regional inequality, public infrastructure, and the future of mountain economies.
