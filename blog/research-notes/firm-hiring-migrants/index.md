### Why Do Firms Hire Migrant Workers?

Hiring decisions are usually discussed from the perspective of workers, but firms also have preferences over whom they employ. Employers may care about reliability, skills, previous experience, wage demands, and whether workers are likely to remain in the job. These considerations can become especially important in informal labour markets where contracts and formal screening mechanisms are limited.

Migrant workers can also face an information disadvantage. They may know less about local employers and working conditions, while employers may know less about workers' backgrounds. Understanding these information gaps can help explain why workers and firms sometimes make decisions that look surprising from the outside.
