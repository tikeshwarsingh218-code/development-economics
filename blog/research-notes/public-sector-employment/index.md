### Public-Sector Employment and the Search for Government Jobs

Government employment is an important part of the aspirations of many young workers in India. Public-sector jobs can offer relatively predictable employment, wages, benefits, and social status. At the same time, recruitment processes can involve substantial competition, repeated examinations, long preparation periods, and uncertainty about when vacancies will become available.

I am interested in the behaviour of people who spend considerable time preparing for public-sector employment. The search process itself raises questions about information, expectations, opportunity costs, and how people respond to uncertainty. Understanding these choices can tell us something about labour-market allocation as well as the broader demand for secure employment.
