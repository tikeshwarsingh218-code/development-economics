### Labour Markets in India

India's labour market is interesting partly because the same economy contains very different kinds of work. A worker may have a regular salaried job, work as a casual labourer, run a small household enterprise, or combine several activities across the year. This makes simple measures of employment difficult to interpret. I am particularly interested in how workers move between these forms of employment, how wages and working conditions differ across them, and how local economic opportunities shape these choices.

The Periodic Labour Force Survey provides an important starting point for studying these questions because it allows us to look at employment, unemployment, labour-force participation, and the characteristics of workers and their jobs. I am interested in the relationship between informality, migration, urbanisation, and development, and in understanding the constraints that shape the choices workers make.
