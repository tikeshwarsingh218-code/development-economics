### Why Do Firms Hire Migrant Workers?

**Research Intern**  
Centre for the Study of the Indian Economy (CSIE), Azim Premji University  
Patna, Bihar  
June–July 2025

**Supervisor:** Prof. Chinmaya Kumar (Azim Premji University, Bengaluru)

### Fieldwork

Worked on *Why Do Firms Hire Migrant Workers?*, a cross-randomized field experiment examining employer hiring preferences in informal labor markets.

Assisted and supervised 5 enumerators during the pilot survey and facilitated interviews with 25 construction contractors and 15 households to collect primary data using SurveyCTO.

Assisted with questionnaire design and piloting, reviewed and refined enumerator training materials, trained 5 enumerators, and conducted audio audits for data quality during implementation.
