### What is the Coase Theorem?

The Coase Theorem describes a setting in which parties affected by an externality can potentially reach an efficient outcome through bargaining when property rights are clearly defined and transaction costs are sufficiently low.

The result highlights the importance of transaction costs and institutions. In real world settings, bargaining may be difficult because many affected parties are involved, information is incomplete, or negotiating and enforcing agreements is costly.
