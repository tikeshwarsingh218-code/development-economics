### What is omitted variable bias?

Omitted variable bias occurs when an important variable is left out of a regression and that variable is related both to the outcome and to an included explanatory variable. The coefficient on the included variable can then capture part of the effect of the omitted factor.

The direction and size of omitted variable bias depend on the relationships among the variables. Adding controls can reduce the problem when the controls are appropriate, but researchers need to consider whether the additional variables themselves are affected by the treatment or outcome.
