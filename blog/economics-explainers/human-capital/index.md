### What is human capital?

Human capital refers to knowledge, skills, health, and other characteristics that affect a person's productivity and economic opportunities. Education and training are common ways through which human capital can be accumulated.

Human capital can affect earnings, employment, productivity, and economic growth. Its effects depend not only on the quantity of education or training but also on quality and how well skills match available economic opportunities.
