### What is a public good?

A public good is typically defined by non rivalry and non excludability. Non rivalry means that one person's use does not reduce the amount available to others, while non excludability means that it is difficult to prevent people from using the good.

These characteristics can create problems for private provision because individuals may have incentives to benefit without contributing to the cost. Public goods therefore provide an important setting for studying collective action and government intervention.
