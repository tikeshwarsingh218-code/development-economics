### What is survey weighting?

Survey weighting assigns different importance to observations so that the sample can represent the population from which it was drawn. An observation that represents many people in the population receives a larger weight than an observation representing fewer people.

Weights are determined by the survey design and may also incorporate adjustments for nonresponse or other factors. Using the correct weights is important when estimating population level quantities from complex surveys.
