### What is selection bias?

Selection bias occurs when the units observed in one group differ systematically from the units observed in another group in ways that matter for the outcome. A simple comparison can then combine the effect of the treatment with pre existing differences between groups.

Random assignment is one way to reduce selection bias because treatment status is determined independently of potential outcomes. In observational research, researchers need alternative strategies to address differences between treated and comparison groups.
