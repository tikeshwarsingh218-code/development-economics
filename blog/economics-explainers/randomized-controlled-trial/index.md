### What is a Randomized Controlled Trial?

A Randomized Controlled Trial assigns units to treatment and comparison groups through a random process. Because assignment is random, differences between groups after the intervention can be interpreted as causal under appropriate implementation conditions.

Randomization does not automatically guarantee a successful experiment. Attrition, noncompliance, spillovers, weak implementation, and measurement problems can affect the estimated treatment effect. These issues must therefore be considered when interpreting experimental results.
