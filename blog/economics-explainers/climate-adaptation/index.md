### What is climate adaptation?

Climate adaptation refers to actions that reduce the harm caused by current or expected climate impacts. Households, firms, communities, and governments can adapt through changes in infrastructure, technology, behaviour, land use, and public policy.

Adaptation opportunities differ across locations and households. Access to finance, information, infrastructure, technology, and public institutions can determine how effectively people respond to climate related risks.
