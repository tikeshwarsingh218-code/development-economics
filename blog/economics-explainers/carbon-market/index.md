### What is a carbon market?

A carbon market creates a system in which emissions or emissions reductions are assigned an economic value. Depending on the design, firms may trade permits or credits associated with greenhouse gas emissions or reductions.

The objective is to create incentives for reducing emissions while allowing flexibility in how reductions are achieved. The effectiveness of a carbon market depends on measurement, monitoring, enforcement, market design, and the credibility of the underlying emissions units.
