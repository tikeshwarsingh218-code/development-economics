### What is internal migration?

Internal migration occurs when people move from one location to another within the same country. The movement can be temporary, seasonal, circular, or permanent and can occur for employment, education, family reasons, or other purposes.

Internal migration connects local labour markets and can redistribute workers across regions and sectors. Understanding it requires attention to both origin and destination conditions as well as the costs associated with moving.
