### What is an externality?

An externality occurs when an action by one person or firm affects the welfare of another person without that effect being fully reflected in the market price. Pollution is a common example because production can impose costs on people who are not directly involved in the transaction.

Externalities can lead to inefficient outcomes because private decisions do not account for the full social costs or benefits. Policy responses can include taxes, regulation, property rights, or other institutional arrangements.
