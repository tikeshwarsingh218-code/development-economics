### What are fixed effects?

Fixed effects allow researchers to account for unobserved characteristics that are constant within a unit over time. For example, a panel dataset can use individual fixed effects to remove time invariant differences between individuals.

Fixed effects can be useful when those unobserved characteristics are correlated with explanatory variables. They do not, however, solve every source of omitted variable bias because characteristics that change over time remain relevant.
