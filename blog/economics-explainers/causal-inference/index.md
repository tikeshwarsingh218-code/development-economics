### What is causal inference?

Causal inference asks what would happen to the same unit under different possible conditions. The central challenge is that researchers cannot normally observe both the treated and untreated outcome for the same unit at the same time.

Research designs attempt to construct credible comparisons that approximate this missing counterfactual. Experiments, natural experiments, Difference in Differences, instrumental variables, and other methods use different assumptions to address this problem.
