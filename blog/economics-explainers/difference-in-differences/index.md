### What is Difference in Differences?

Difference in Differences compares changes in outcomes over time between a treated group and a comparison group. The comparison is based on the idea that the comparison group can provide information about the change that would have occurred for the treated group without the intervention.

The key identifying assumption is that the groups would have followed similar trends in the absence of treatment. Researchers therefore pay close attention to pre treatment trends and to whether other events affected the groups differently.
