### What is labour market informality?

Labour market informality refers to employment relationships that operate outside some formal institutional arrangements. Informal workers may lack written contracts, social protection, regulated working conditions, or other features associated with formal employment.

Informality can arise from many sources, including firm size, regulation, enforcement, productivity, worker characteristics, and labour market institutions. It is therefore useful to examine the specific form of informality rather than treating all informal employment as identical.
