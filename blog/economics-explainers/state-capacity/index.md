### What is state capacity?

State capacity is the ability of public institutions to perform core functions such as raising revenue, enforcing rules, collecting information, and delivering public services. It can vary across countries, regions, agencies, and levels of government.

Strong administrative systems can make it easier to implement policies consistently. Weak capacity can create gaps between policies as written and the services or outcomes experienced by citizens.
