### What is spatial inequality?

Spatial inequality refers to differences in economic and social outcomes across geographic areas. Regions can differ in income, employment, education, health, infrastructure, public services, and access to markets.

These differences can persist because geography interacts with institutions, historical development, investment, migration, and economic opportunities. Studying spatial inequality therefore requires attention to both local conditions and broader economic processes.
