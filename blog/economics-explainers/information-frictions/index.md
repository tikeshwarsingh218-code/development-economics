### What are information frictions?

Information frictions occur when people do not have complete or accurate information when making economic decisions. Workers may not know about available jobs, firms may not know the characteristics of applicants, and consumers may not know the quality of products.

These frictions can prevent mutually beneficial exchanges from occurring. Networks, information platforms, search mechanisms, and public disclosure can sometimes reduce the costs created by incomplete information.
