### What is a sampling frame?

A sampling frame is the list or structure from which units are selected into a sample. It may contain households, individuals, firms, schools, geographic areas, or other units depending on the research design.

The quality of a sampling frame affects the quality of the resulting sample. Missing units, duplicates, outdated information, and systematic coverage differences can create problems even when the sampling procedure itself is correctly implemented.
