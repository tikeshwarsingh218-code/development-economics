### Matching districts across Census years

District names cannot always be used directly to match administrative units across Census years. A district may have the same name but different boundaries, while two different names may refer to areas that later become part of the same district.

Matching districts therefore requires combining names, geographic boundaries, codes, and information about district creation and division. The objective is to establish a consistent relationship between geographic units while preserving information about changes in administrative boundaries.
