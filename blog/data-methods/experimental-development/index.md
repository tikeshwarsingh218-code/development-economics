### Experimental research in development economics

Experimental research in development economics studies how policies, institutions, information, and incentives affect economic behaviour. Researchers can use experiments to test specific mechanisms and estimate the effects of interventions in real world settings.

Good experimental research begins with a clearly defined question and a credible design. The researcher must also consider implementation, measurement, ethical issues, statistical power, and whether the setting allows the findings to be applied to other populations or contexts.
