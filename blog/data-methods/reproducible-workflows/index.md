### Building reproducible research workflows

A reproducible research workflow makes it possible to understand how raw data were transformed into the results presented in a research project. This requires organised files, documented code, consistent naming conventions, and clear separation between raw data and processed outputs.

Reproducibility also reduces the risk of errors during repeated analysis. When cleaning, analysis, tables, and figures are generated through documented scripts, changes can be made systematically and the same analysis can be rerun when new data or corrections become available.
