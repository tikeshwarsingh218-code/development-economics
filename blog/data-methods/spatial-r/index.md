### Working with spatial data in R

R provides a flexible environment for working with geographic data. Spatial objects can be read, transformed, joined, intersected, and visualised within a reproducible workflow. This makes it possible to combine administrative records with geographic boundaries and coordinate data.

Spatial analysis also requires attention to coordinate reference systems. Distances, areas, intersections, and maps can produce incorrect results when geographic objects use incompatible systems. A careful workflow therefore checks projections and geometry before performing spatial operations.
