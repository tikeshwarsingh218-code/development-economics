### Pilot surveys and enumerator training

A pilot survey provides an opportunity to test the questionnaire and field procedures before the main data collection begins. It can reveal problems with question wording, survey length, respondent understanding, sampling procedures, and technical implementation.

Enumerator training is equally important because the way a question is presented can affect the response. Training should establish consistent procedures while preparing enumerators to handle questions, unexpected situations, consent, and data quality issues during fieldwork.
