### Extracting information from PDFs

Many government records and historical documents are distributed as PDF files rather than structured datasets. Extracting information from these documents can involve identifying tables, reading text, handling scanned pages, and preserving the relationship between different parts of a document.

Automated extraction can greatly reduce repetitive work, but extracted information needs to be checked against the original documents. Tables can be misread, characters can be confused, and scanned documents may require additional processing. Validation is therefore essential when building a research dataset from PDFs.
