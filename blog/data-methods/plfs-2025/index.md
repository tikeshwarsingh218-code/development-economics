### The redesigned PLFS from 2025

The redesigned PLFS from 2025 changes important aspects of the survey structure and data collection system. Understanding these changes is necessary when working with the newer rounds because variables, schedules, sampling arrangements, and the organisation of the unit level data may differ from earlier versions.

Researchers using the redesigned survey need to distinguish changes in the underlying labour market from changes introduced by the survey redesign. Careful documentation of variables and survey concepts is therefore important when comparing the new data with earlier rounds.
