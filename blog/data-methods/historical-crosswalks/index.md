### Constructing historical geographic crosswalks

Historical geographic crosswalks allow researchers to translate information from one set of administrative boundaries into another. They are particularly useful when datasets collected in different periods use different district or constituency boundaries.

A crosswalk can be constructed using geographic overlap between historical and current polygons. The resulting relationship can then be represented either as a dominant match or as shares of the source area associated with different target units. The choice depends on the research question and the information available.
