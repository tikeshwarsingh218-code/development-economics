### Periodic Labour Force Survey

The Periodic Labour Force Survey is an important source for studying employment, unemployment, labour force participation, and the structure of work in India. It provides information about the labour market characteristics of individuals and households and allows researchers to examine differences across states, demographic groups, and types of employment.

Working with the PLFS requires careful attention to the survey structure, variables, weights, and definitions used to classify labour market activity. The data can be used to study employment patterns, unemployment, labour force participation, wages, migration, and differences between rural and urban labour markets.
