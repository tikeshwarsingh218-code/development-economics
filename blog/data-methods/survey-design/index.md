### Survey design and questionnaire development

A survey questionnaire translates a research question into measurable variables. Good questionnaire design requires deciding what information is necessary, how questions should be worded, what response options should be available, and how questions should be ordered.

Poorly designed questions can introduce measurement error even when data collection is implemented correctly. Piloting helps identify confusing wording, missing response categories, inappropriate skip patterns, and questions that respondents find difficult to answer.
