### Randomized Controlled Trials

A Randomized Controlled Trial assigns individuals, households, schools, firms, or other units to different groups using a random process. Random assignment helps ensure that treatment and comparison groups are similar on average before the intervention takes place.

The difference in outcomes between the groups can then provide evidence about the causal effect of the intervention. Careful implementation remains important because attrition, noncompliance, spillovers, and measurement problems can affect the interpretation of experimental results.
