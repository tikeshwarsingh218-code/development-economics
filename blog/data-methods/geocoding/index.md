### Geocoding schools and administrative records

Geocoding converts names and addresses into geographic coordinates that can be used for spatial analysis. Administrative datasets often contain locations in inconsistent formats, making automated geocoding difficult. Names may contain spelling differences, abbreviations, missing information, or historical place names.

A reliable geocoding workflow therefore combines automated services with cleaning and validation. Results can be checked using geographic boundaries, nearby locations, postal information, and alternative sources. Records that cannot be confidently matched should remain identifiable as unresolved rather than being assigned arbitrary coordinates.
