### Difference in Differences

Difference in Differences is a method for estimating the effect of an intervention by comparing changes over time between a treated group and a comparison group. The central idea is that the change experienced by the comparison group provides information about what might have happened to the treated group without the intervention.

The credibility of the method depends heavily on the assumptions behind the comparison. One important condition is that the groups would have followed similar trends in the absence of treatment. Researchers therefore examine pre treatment trends and carefully define the treatment, comparison group, and time periods.
