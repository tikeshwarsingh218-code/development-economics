### Understanding the PLFS sampling design

The PLFS uses a complex sampling design rather than a simple random sample of individuals. Households are selected through multiple stages, and the design incorporates geographical units, stratification, and sampling probabilities. Understanding these stages is necessary for interpreting estimates correctly.

Survey weights allow observations in the sample to represent the wider population from which they were selected. Researchers also need to account for clustering and stratification when estimating standard errors. Ignoring these features can produce misleading measures of precision.
