### SurveyCTO and field data collection

SurveyCTO can be used to program questionnaires, collect responses electronically, implement skip patterns, and monitor fieldwork. Digital data collection can reduce some forms of manual data entry and allow researchers to build validation rules directly into the survey instrument.

The quality of the final dataset still depends on field implementation. Enumerator training, pilot testing, regular checks, and careful monitoring are necessary to identify problems while data collection is taking place rather than after the survey has ended.
