### Working with QGIS

QGIS is useful for inspecting, cleaning, joining, and visualising spatial data. It provides a graphical environment in which geographic boundaries, points, and other spatial layers can be examined directly. This is especially useful when checking the results of geocoding or spatial matching.

QGIS can also support research workflows that combine visual inspection with statistical analysis. Researchers can identify unusual locations, examine boundary changes, verify geographic relationships, and prepare spatial files before taking them into R or another statistical environment.
