### Starting with a research question

A research project begins with a question that can be stated clearly enough to guide the collection and analysis of evidence. A useful question identifies the outcome of interest, the relevant population or setting, and the relationship that the researcher wants to understand.

The question also determines what kind of data and research design are appropriate. Spending time refining the question before beginning analysis can prevent large amounts of work on data or methods that do not answer the underlying research problem.
