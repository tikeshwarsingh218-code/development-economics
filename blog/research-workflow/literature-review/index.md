### Finding and organising the literature

A literature review helps establish what is already known about a research question and where important gaps remain. It involves identifying relevant theoretical arguments, empirical findings, datasets, and methodological approaches.

Organising the literature systematically makes it easier to compare studies and identify differences in questions, settings, data, and identification strategies. Good notes should capture the main contribution of a study as well as the assumptions behind its conclusions.
