### From analysis to research writing

Research writing connects the empirical analysis to the question that motivated the project. The results should be presented in a way that makes the identification strategy, evidence, and limitations understandable to the reader.

Writing can also reveal weaknesses in the analysis. Explaining what a coefficient means, why a comparison is credible, and what alternative explanations remain can help identify areas where additional analysis is needed.
