### Documenting research decisions

Research projects involve many decisions about data construction, variable definitions, exclusions, coding, and analysis. If these decisions are not recorded, it becomes difficult to understand why the final results look the way they do.

Documentation can take the form of code comments, readme files, data dictionaries, research notes, and version controlled scripts. The goal is to make the reasoning behind the analysis recoverable.
