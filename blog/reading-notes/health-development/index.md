### Health and development

Health affects people's ability to work, study, participate in economic activity, and live productive lives. Poor health can reduce household income while also creating substantial expenditure on treatment and care.

Health outcomes are influenced by income, nutrition, sanitation, healthcare access, environmental conditions, and public institutions. Development analysis therefore needs to consider health both as an outcome and as an important input into economic wellbeing.
