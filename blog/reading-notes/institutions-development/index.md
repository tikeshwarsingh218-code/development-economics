### Institutions and economic development

Institutions shape the incentives faced by households, firms, and governments. Rules concerning property, contracts, taxation, political participation, and public administration can influence how economic decisions are made and how resources are allocated.

Institutional differences can therefore help explain why economies with similar resources can experience different development outcomes. Studying institutions requires attention to both formal rules and the informal practices through which those rules operate.
