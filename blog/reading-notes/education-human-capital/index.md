### Education and human capital

Education can increase knowledge, skills, productivity, and access to employment opportunities. Human capital is therefore an important component of economic development and can affect both individual earnings and broader economic outcomes.

The effects of education depend on the quality and relevance of learning as well as years of schooling. Access, school quality, teacher availability, and household circumstances can all influence whether additional schooling translates into improved outcomes.
