### Climate change and development

Climate change can affect development through agricultural productivity, health, infrastructure, migration, and local employment. These effects are often particularly important for households whose livelihoods depend directly on environmental conditions.

Adaptation can reduce some of these risks, but the ability to adapt differs across households and locations. Economic resources, public infrastructure, information, and institutional support can all influence how communities respond to climate change.
