### State capacity and development

State capacity concerns the ability of governments to collect information, implement policies, raise revenue, enforce rules, and provide public services. These functions are central to the functioning of modern economies and can affect development outcomes directly.

Capacity is not uniform across governments or even within the same government. Local institutions, frontline workers, administrative systems, and available resources can all influence how effectively policies are implemented.
