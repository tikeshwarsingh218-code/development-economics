### Environment and economic development

Economic activity depends on natural resources and environmental systems, while production and consumption can also create pollution and other environmental costs. Development policy therefore involves choices about how resources are used and how environmental damages are managed.

Environmental problems often involve externalities because the costs created by one person's actions can affect others. Understanding these problems requires combining economic incentives with information about environmental conditions and institutional capacity.
