### Markets and development

Markets coordinate decisions made by households and firms through prices and exchange. In developing economies, markets can expand access to goods, employment, finance, and technology, but they may also function imperfectly because information and infrastructure are limited.

Understanding development therefore requires examining both the benefits of markets and the conditions required for markets to work well. Transaction costs, market power, missing markets, and information problems can all affect economic outcomes.
