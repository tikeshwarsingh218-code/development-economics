### Migration and development

Migration can be an important mechanism through which households respond to differences in economic opportunities across locations. Workers may move temporarily or permanently in response to wages, employment opportunities, household needs, or economic shocks.

Migration can affect both origin and destination economies. Remittances can support households in origin areas, while migrant workers can contribute labour to destination economies. The effects depend on the characteristics of migrants and the institutions through which migration takes place.
