### Labour markets and development

Labour markets connect workers who supply their time and skills with firms and other organisations that demand labour. In developing economies, these markets are often divided across formal and informal employment and across agriculture, manufacturing, construction, and services.

Changes in labour markets are closely connected to structural transformation. As economies develop, workers may move across sectors, occupations, locations, and types of employment, creating important consequences for productivity, wages, and household welfare.
