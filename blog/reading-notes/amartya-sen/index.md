### Amartya Sen and development

Amartya Sen's work provides a way of thinking about development that goes beyond income and economic growth. Development can be understood in terms of the expansion of people's capabilities and their ability to lead lives that they have reason to value.

This perspective places freedom, opportunity, health, education, and access to resources at the centre of development analysis. It also highlights why economic development cannot be evaluated only through aggregate income or production.
