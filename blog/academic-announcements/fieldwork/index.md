### Fieldwork

Fieldwork provides an opportunity to understand how institutions, markets, and policies operate in practice. Interviews, surveys, observations, and conversations with participants can reveal information that may not be visible in administrative or survey datasets alone.

Good fieldwork requires careful preparation and documentation. Questionnaire design, enumerator training, consent, piloting, data quality checks, and field protocols all influence the quality of the resulting evidence.
