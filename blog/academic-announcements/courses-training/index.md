### Courses and training

Courses and training provide a structured way to build knowledge in economic theory, empirical methods, and specialised research areas. I use them to strengthen foundations in development economics, labour economics, political economy, environmental economics, and quantitative methods.

Training is particularly useful when it connects theoretical concepts with empirical applications. Working through research papers, datasets, problem sets, and applied exercises helps turn concepts into practical research skills.
